What is Pressed Peanut Candy?

Pressed Peanut Candy is a peanut-based snack made from roasted red peanuts, sugar, and salt. The peanuts are processed into a powdery-like consistency before being pressed into a compact and convenient shape.

Each piece is individually packaged to provide cleanliness, convenience, and easier handling for consumers who want a simple yet satisfying snack.

It is intended for local consumers in Ormoc City, Leyte who enjoy straightforward, affordable treats made with familiar ingredients.
